Aave Loan Checker

Paste or scan a public wallet address to see its Aave V3 loan, health factor and liquidation price. Works for any address, nothing to install.

Nothing is saved on a server. Clear removes it from this browser. Your browser history may still show the link.

Tip: if your loan is held by a Safe or smart wallet, paste that contract address, not the owner wallet. You can also share a link like ?address=0x...&chain=base.

What the health factor means

    Health factor is your collateral (weighted by each asset's liquidation threshold) divided by your debt. Below 1.0 your collateral can be liquidated.

    Read-only. This page never asks for a seed phrase or private key and never sends a transaction. It only reads public blockchain data.

    Not financial advice. Numbers are estimates from on-chain data and can change quickly. Always check the official Aave app before acting.
