Guide, sharing and tips

What the health factor means

    Health factor is your collateral (weighted by each asset's liquidation threshold) divided by your debt. Below 1.0 your collateral can be liquidated.

    Tips

    If your loan is held by a Safe or smart wallet, paste that contract address, not the owner wallet. You can also share a link like ?address=0x...&chain=base.

    Nothing is saved on a server. Clear removes it from this browser. Your browser history may still show the link.

    Read-only. This page never asks for a seed phrase or private key and never sends a transaction. It only reads public blockchain data.
